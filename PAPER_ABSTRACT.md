# Paper Abstract

## 中文摘要

本文研究 Future Attention (FA) 与 Expected Free Energy (EFE) 之间的关系，但不预设两者等价。我们考虑一个固定的 causal Transformer world model，在一个可精确计算 EFE 的小型稳态 POMDP 中，将未来 self-attention 对当前候选动作的回指强度定义为 `oracle FA`，并训练一个轻量预测器在在线决策时近似该信号。基于这一设置，我们比较了 `oracle FA`、学习后的 `predictor FA` 与 `Exact EFE` 的动作排序一致性，并进一步研究了闭环更新下 `policy -> data -> attention target -> predictor -> policy` 的相互作用。结果表明：在全可见条件下，attention-defined FA 与 Exact EFE 存在中等程度的动作排序对齐；在部分可观测条件下，这种对齐明显下降，并且会受到 rollout policy 与分布漂移的显著影响。额外对照显示，固定的信息敏感 rollout policy 可以略微改善 FA 与 EFE 的排序一致性，但可能牺牲行为回报。上述结果支持一个更收缩但更稳健的结论：未来自注意力可以在固定 world model 中诱导一个可学习、可用于控制的前瞻信号，但该信号与 EFE 的关系是条件性的、近似性的，而非一般性的等价。

## English Abstract

This paper studies the relationship between Future Attention (FA) and Expected Free Energy (EFE) without assuming that the two are equivalent. We consider a fixed causal Transformer world model in a small homeostatic POMDP where Exact EFE is tractable, and define `oracle FA` as the future self-attention assigned back to a current candidate action during imagined rollouts. A lightweight predictor is then trained to approximate this signal for online control. Within this setup, we compare the action rankings induced by `oracle FA`, learned `predictor FA`, and `Exact EFE`, and analyze the closed-loop interaction among `policy -> data -> attention target -> predictor -> policy`. The results show moderate action-ranking alignment between attention-defined FA and Exact EFE under full observability, but substantially weaker alignment under partial observability, where rollout policy and distribution shift become major factors. Additional controls indicate that a fixed information-sensitive rollout policy can slightly improve FA-EFE ranking agreement, but often at the cost of behavioral return. These findings support a narrower but stronger conclusion: future self-attention can induce a learnable and behaviorally useful prospective signal in a fixed world model, yet its relationship to EFE is conditional and approximate rather than generally equivalent.
